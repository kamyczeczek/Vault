## Verdict — Is planning ICM-sound?

**No — planning is the weakest ICM layer in this project.** The filesystem does carry the plan (numbered `stages/01..04`, each with a one-job `CONTEXT.md` Inputs Table; `pipeline/06_reflect/WORKFLOW.md` is the only explicit end-to-end plan). That satisfies ICM's "filesystem as orchestrator / one stage one job" at a coarse level (`00_icm_map`, `00_interpretable_context_methodology_folder_structu`). But against the planning literature in the injected graph, three ICM planning obligations are missing: (1) no global plan artifact with ordered phases and acceptance gates that an agent must demonstrably follow (`00_from_plan_to_action_how_well_do_agents_follow_th`); (2) no adaptive decomposition — plans are static, never recursively split on executor failure (`00_adapt_as_needed_decomposition_and_planning_with`); (3) no plan persistence/reminder, so context-window pressure will erode compliance on long-horizon tasks (same source). A fourth ICM gap is that planning is untracked as a topic — `knowledge/topics.md` lists `planning` but no digest or candidate set owns it. Fixable without architecture change.
